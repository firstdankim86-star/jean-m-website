WHO WE ARE

Called to serve Christ and His people

Jesus Anointed Ministry (JEAN-M) is a Christian faith ministry based in Kilifi, Kenya Coast. Our desire is to see individuals and families encounter Jesus Christ, mature in faith, and become a blessing to their communities.

We believe the local church and Christian ministry should be a place where the Word of God is taught faithfully, prayer is encouraged, worship is genuine, and every person is treated with dignity and love.

01

Our Mission

To proclaim Jesus Christ and make disciples through biblical teaching, prayer, worship, evangelism and compassionate service.

02

Our Vision

To see transformed lives and Christ-centered families advancing God's Kingdom in Kenya and beyond.
